About the App

LullaCam is a local-network baby monitor. One compatible Apple device acts as a Camera and another compatible Apple device acts as a Monitor. The Camera captures live video and audio, which is transmitted to the Monitor.

LullaCam does not require an account or registration to use its core monitoring functionality.
