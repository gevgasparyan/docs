About the App

BarKap (ԲառԿապ) is a word-connection puzzle in Armenian. Every board hides groups of words that belong together. Drag a word onto another to swap them, and line up the words of one group in a single row to reveal its theme.

  • 700+ Armenian words in 60+ themes
  • Four difficulty levels, from easy to expert
  • A fresh daily challenge and daily rewards
  • Hints when you're stuck, and points for clearing levels
  • No account or sign-up, and it plays offline
