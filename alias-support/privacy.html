Privacy Policy

Alias

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Alias ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. Alias is a party word-guessing game. It has no accounts or logins, and team names and game results stay on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts. Some data is collected automatically by the third-party services the App uses to show ads, deliver updates, and measure app performance:

  • Device identifiers: the device ID used for ads (identifier for vendor on iOS, advertising ID on Android) and a random per-install ID used for app updates and usage counts.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address by our ad provider. The App never asks for or uses your precise (GPS) location.
  • Ad and usage data: which ads were shown and tapped, app launches, and taps.
  • Performance and diagnostics: app start-up and screen load times, crash, and error data.
  • App and device info: app version, platform, device model, and operating system version.

The App does not:

  • Require an account, login, or any personal details
  • Access your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos
  • Ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites
  • Offer in-app purchases or subscriptions, or process payments

Advertising

Alias is free and shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Google may use the data listed above to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, and prevent fraud. On Android, ads may be personalized using your device's advertising ID; you can reset it or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. On iOS, the App does not request permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so it never receives your advertising identifier (IDFA). Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Expo Updates, Expo Insights, and Expo Observe (650 Industries, Inc.): over-the-air app updates, usage counts, and performance monitoring. Expo processes this data on our behalf. See the Expo Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.): the App downloads word lists and translations from GitHub. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub. No other data is sent. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

All data sent by the App is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data.

Data Storage

Your settings, downloaded word lists, and game data (such as teams and scores) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

Your Choices

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the App to stop all data collection and delete local data.

Because we have no accounts and do not hold data that identifies you, requests about data held by Google or Expo should go to them through the policies linked above. You can also contact us below.

Children's Privacy

Alias is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
