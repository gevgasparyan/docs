Privacy Policy

BarKap

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how BarKap ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. BarKap is a word puzzle game. It has no accounts or logins, and nothing you type in the game is sent anywhere.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts. Some data is collected automatically by the third-party services the App uses to show ads, deliver updates, and count app launches:

  • Device identifiers: the device ID used for ads (identifier for vendor on iOS, advertising ID on Android) and a random per-install ID used for app updates and launch counts.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address by our ad provider. The App never asks for or uses your precise (GPS) location.
  • Ad and usage data: which ads were shown and tapped, app launches, and taps.
  • Diagnostics: crash, performance, and error data.
  • App and device info: app version, platform, and operating system version.

The App does not:

  • Require an account, login, or any personal details
  • Access your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos
  • Track you across other companies' apps or websites
  • Show personalized ads
  • Offer in-app purchases or subscriptions, or process payments

Advertising

BarKap is free and shows ads (banner, full-screen, and optional rewarded video ads) through Google AdMob. The App requests non-personalized ads only and does not request permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so on iOS it never receives your advertising identifier (IDFA). Google may still use the data listed above to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, we ask for your consent where the law requires it. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Expo Updates and Expo Insights (650 Industries, Inc.): over-the-air app updates and anonymous launch counts. Expo processes this data on our behalf. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

All data sent by the App is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your data.

Notifications

With your permission, the App can show daily reminders to come back and play. These are scheduled on your device; no push server is used and no data is sent. You can turn them off in the App's Settings or in your device settings at any time.

Data Storage

Your game progress, points, and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

Your Choices

  • Reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Android), or limit ad tracking.
  • Turn off reminders in the App's Settings or your device settings.
  • Uninstall the App to stop all data collection and delete local data.

Because we have no accounts and do not hold data that identifies you, requests about data held by Google or Expo should go to them through the policies linked above. You can also contact us below.

Children's Privacy

BarKap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
