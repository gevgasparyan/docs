Privacy Policy

LullaCam

Last updated: September 27, 2026

LullaCam is developed and operated by Gevorg Gasparyan ("I", "me", "my", or "Developer").

This Privacy Policy explains how LullaCam ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

1. Overview

LullaCam is designed as a local-network baby monitor.

The App allows one compatible Apple device to act as a Camera and another compatible Apple device to act as a Monitor.

The Camera captures live video and audio, which is transmitted to the Monitor for the purpose of providing the monitoring functionality.

LullaCam does not require an account or registration to use its core monitoring functionality.

2. Camera and Microphone

When you use a device as a Camera, LullaCam requests access to:

  • Camera — to capture live video.
  • Microphone — to capture live audio.

Camera and microphone data are used to provide live monitoring between the Camera and Monitor devices.

The live baby-monitor video and audio stream is not uploaded to or stored on the Developer's servers as a cloud recording.

Camera and microphone access can be controlled through the privacy settings on your Apple device.

3. Local Network

LullaCam requests Local Network access to discover and communicate with other LullaCam devices on the same local network.

The App uses local network technologies to discover the Camera and Monitor devices and establish the monitoring connection.

Live monitoring is designed to take place directly between the participating devices over the local network.

4. Network and Device Information

LullaCam may access technical information required for the App to establish and maintain a local connection, such as network configuration information, device connectivity status, and information necessary to discover compatible devices on the local network.

This information is used to provide the App's functionality.

5. Personal Information

LullaCam does not require you to create an account.

The core monitoring functionality does not require you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, home address, or similar personal information.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

LullaCam offers optional subscriptions and may offer a lifetime purchase through Apple's App Store.

Payments are processed through Apple's App Store.

LullaCam uses Apphud to help manage and verify subscription and purchase status.

Apphud may process information associated with purchases and subscriptions, as well as technical information necessary to provide its service.

The Developer does not receive your payment card number or other payment credentials.

Apple processes payment information according to Apple's privacy policy and terms.

7. Third-Party Services

LullaCam may use the following third-party services:

Apple

Apple services, including the App Store and StoreKit, are used to process purchases and subscriptions and provide App Store functionality.

Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and terms.

Apphud

LullaCam uses Apphud to manage subscription and purchase information and determine entitlement status.

Apphud may process technical information and purchase-related information necessary to provide its services.

For more information, please refer to Apphud's applicable privacy documentation.

9. Video and Audio Data

LullaCam's core monitoring functionality involves live video and audio captured by the Camera device.

This data is transmitted to the Monitor device to provide live monitoring.

The Developer does not intentionally collect, store, or use this live baby-monitor video or audio for advertising or analytics.

The live stream is not retained by LullaCam as a cloud recording.

10. Children's Privacy

LullaCam is intended for use by parents and caregivers.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The Camera functionality may capture images or audio of a child during a monitoring session. This content is used to provide the monitoring functionality and is transmitted to the Monitor device selected by the user.

The Developer does not intentionally collect or store this content as a cloud recording.

11. Data Storage and Retention

LullaCam does not store live baby-monitor video or audio on the Developer's servers.

Information stored locally by the App, such as settings or preferences, remains on the user's device.

Purchase and subscription information may be processed or retained by Apple and Apphud according to their applicable policies and service requirements.

12. Security

LullaCam uses technical measures appropriate to its functionality to protect communications between devices.

However, no electronic communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for using a trusted local network and maintaining appropriate security on their devices.

13. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, LullaCam may request access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Local Network

The App may also access device or network information necessary to provide its functionality.

You can manage applicable permissions through your Apple device's Settings.

14. Your Choices

You can control access to the Camera, Microphone, and Local Network through your device's privacy settings.

You can stop using LullaCam and delete the App from your device at any time.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID account settings.

15. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may be shown a privacy or consent message when using LullaCam. These choices allow you to control how information may be used for advertising and related purposes where required by applicable law.

You can also manage certain privacy and advertising settings through your device settings.

Where applicable, LullaCam respects your choices regarding advertising personalization and applicable privacy regulations.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or data practices change.

The updated version will be published at the same URL and will include an updated "Last updated" date.
